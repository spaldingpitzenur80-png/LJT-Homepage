I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/Vicent0205) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present) under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree (2020-2024), where I was also previously advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML).
